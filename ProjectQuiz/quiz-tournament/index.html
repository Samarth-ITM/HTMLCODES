About the Format

Welcome to the ultimate coding quiz tournament! Our platform is designed to test your programming knowledge, speed, and accuracy in a highly competitive environment. Here's how it works:

Time Attack Mode

Every question is timed. The faster you answer correctly, the more points you earn. Hesitation will cost you position on the leaderboard.

Dynamic Difficulty

Questions automatically scale based on your performance. A streak of correct answers unlocked harder, high-value questions.

No Alt-Tabbing

Our strict anti-cheat system monitors tab focus. If you leave the quiz window to search for an answer, you will be penalized or disqualified.
